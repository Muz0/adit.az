ADIT is a leading electronics distributor based in Baku, Azerbaijan, specializing in high-quality consumer and industrial electronics. As the official distributor of Samsung, Apple, Philips, and Xiaomi, we provide trusted solutions for businesses, retail networks, and enterprises across Azerbaijan and the wider Caucasus region.

We value quality, transparency, and long-term partnerships—offering not just products, but complete solutions tailored to our clients’ needs.
