Business Partnership Terms

Delivery & Logistics

Orders are delivered directly to the partner’s office or warehouse. Pickup and repair services are provided for defective products covered under the manufacturer’s warranty.

Product Warranty

All products come with official manufacturer warranties. Defective items are repaired or replaced in accordance with warranty terms.

Return & Exchange Policy

Eligible products may be returned or exchanged within a specified period (e.g., 14 days). This process is carried out after technical inspection and approval by the manufacturer’s authorized service center.

Stock Reservation

Partners can reserve products in advance for a specified period by making a deposit payment.

After-Sales Support

We provide ongoing technical assistance, repair services, and professional consultation to ensure customer satisfaction even after the sale.

Contract Duration & Renewal

Partnership agreements are established on an annual basis and can be renewed periodically depending on performance and cooperation.

Confidentiality & Exclusivity (Optional)

In certain partnerships, confidentiality or exclusivity terms may apply based on mutual agreement.

*Prices are provided upon signing a partnership agreement.
